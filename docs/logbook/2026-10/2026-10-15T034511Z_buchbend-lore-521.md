**2026-10-15** · Christof Buchbender · buchbend/lore#521

**Why:** `lore roadmap add "Why?"` wrote `- **Why?.**` into `ROADMAP.md`. The whole-epic review of [#515](https://github.com/buchbend/lore/pull/515) (epic #510, the roadmap) found the case, and [#519](https://github.com/buchbend/lore/issues/519) (keep closing punctuation of a roadmap add title) recorded it.

**What we did:** The command still strips one trailing dot from the title, so a title of one dot stays an empty title. Then the command adds the closing dot only to a title that ends in neither `?` nor `!`. We added one check after the strip and kept the title rule of [#512](https://github.com/buchbend/lore/pull/512) (add `lore roadmap add`). That rule already holds for every other title.

**Consequences:**
- A person who files a question or an exclamation as a roadmap item gets the title as typed.
- Nobody outside this repo notices otherwise.

**Not done yet:**
- Not yet checked: the other line-parsing cases of the section, such as code fences, the case of the heading and CRLF line endings. Issue #519 kept them out of scope.
