**2026-10-15** · Christof Buchbender · buchbend/lore#523

**Why:** [#514](https://github.com/buchbend/lore/pull/514) (teach the workflow skills to read and keep the roadmap) changed the filing rule. A feature idea goes to the roadmap, and a defect goes to an issue. `build` followed the new rule. `handover` still filed every left-over item as an issue. An idea from a stopped session never reached the roadmap ([#517](https://github.com/buchbend/lore/issues/517), deferred feature ideas at a handover).

**What we did:** `handover` now adds a deferred feature idea with `lore roadmap add` in the worktree of the open branch. An idea with findings worth keeping gets a seed issue too, and the roadmap item links the seed. We kept both artifacts, not one, because they hold different things. The roadmap item says where the repo heads. The seed holds what the session learned. Without an open branch, the seed holds the idea alone, and `to-epic` adds the item later. We opened no extra pull request for one roadmap line.

**Consequences:**
- Agents that stop work early file a feature idea on the roadmap, not as an issue.
- Nobody outside this repo notices.
