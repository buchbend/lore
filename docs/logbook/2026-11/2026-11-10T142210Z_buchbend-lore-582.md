**2026-11-10** · Christof Buchbender · buchbend/lore#582

**Why:** [PR #578](https://github.com/buchbend/lore/pull/578) (deliver the SessionStart text whole and point to the handover note) archives the handover note after `/clear`. Its review found three gaps, filed as [#579](https://github.com/buchbend/lore/issues/579) (let `lore handover show` find the note after `/clear`). The command failed after `/clear`. The banner said "handover loaded", and SessionStart loads no note text. A text over 10,000 characters pushed the pointer out of the part that Claude Code shows.

**What we did:** [PR #582](https://github.com/buchbend/lore/pull/582) (let `lore handover show` find the note after `/clear`) makes the command print the newest archived note when no live note exists. The banner now says "handover pointed to". SessionStart places the pointer directly after the status line, not at the end of the text. We moved the pointer and did not let more blocks give way: the first 2,000 characters stay visible whatever the later blocks hold. The pointer does not take line 1, because the banner for the person comes from line 1.

**Consequences:**
- Users: `lore handover show` works after `/clear`, and the banner wording changes.
- Agents: the handover pointer arrives before the directive and the index.
- Nobody outside this repo notices.
