**2026-11-13** · Christof Buchbender · buchbend/lore#608

**Why:** Research, scopings of options, explanations and draft paper material from a session had no home in a repo artifact. The material stayed in the chat and was lost. ADR 0012 (agents file facts as repo artifacts) let no agent write into a wiki on its own.

**What we did:** An agent now offers a wiki entry once, at a stop of the work. After a yes, the agent writes it with the new `/lore:wiki` skill. The yes is the review. The new command `lore wiki commit` checks the entry, commits it and pushes it at once. It opens no pull request, because a pull request is too slow for the live context of a team. The command runs `git init` in a wiki without git, commits hand edits of the person first, and merges the remote first. A conflict keeps the local commit and names the paths ([#608](https://github.com/buchbend/lore/pull/608), wiki entry after a yes).

**Consequences:**
- Users: the agent may ask once whether to keep material as a wiki entry. A yes commits and pushes the entry to the wiki repo.
- Wiki owners: hand edits of a wiki get their own commit when an entry lands.
- Nobody outside this repo notices otherwise.
