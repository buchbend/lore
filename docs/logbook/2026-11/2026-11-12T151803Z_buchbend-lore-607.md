**2026-11-12** · Christof Buchbender · buchbend/lore#607

**Why:** The search index read four fixed folders of a wiki. The wiki `ccat` holds 9 notes in `observatory/` and `systems/`, and the index held none of them. `lore wiki check` already walked every folder.

**What we did:** In [PR 607](https://github.com/buchbend/lore/pull/607) (search walk), the search index and `lore migrate frontmatter` now take their notes from `wiki_notes`, the walk of the check. `discover_notes` and `KNOWLEDGE_DIRS` are gone, so Lore has one walk of a wiki. We deleted the two tests that asserted a skipped `sessions/` folder, because the one walk covers every folder outside the skip list.

**Consequences:**
- Users: `lore_search` finds notes in any folder, except `inbox/`, `templates/`, `style/` and hidden folders.
- Admins: a wiki that holds retired session notes now indexes them.
- Nobody else notices.

**Not done yet:**
- Not yet checked: the size of the index on a wiki with many session notes.
