**2026-11-12** · Christof Buchbender · buchbend/lore#603

**Why:** `lore wiki lint` did three jobs under one name. It wrote catalog and index files into a wiki, it ran ten health checks, and search imported its helpers. Earlier removals left almost no reader for the files. Its structure checks also fixed four folder names, so a wiki could not grow its own structure. The next step, an agent that writes a wiki entry, needs one small check to run first.

**What we did:** [PR #603](https://github.com/buchbend/lore/pull/603) (replace `lore wiki lint` with `lore wiki check` and `lore adr check`) removes the generator and the old checks. `lore wiki check` reads every folder of a wiki and reports a broken link, a missing description and a duplicate filename as errors. It adds the writing rules through Vale. The check asks nothing about size, folder or link count, because a formal structure rule fails in a wiki that grows from use. The three readers of the catalog now read the notes, not a cached file: the catalog of the wiki `ccat` was two months old.

**Consequences:**
- Users: `lore wiki lint` is an unknown command. `lore wiki check` replaces it and writes no file.
- Wiki owners: the files `_catalog.json`, `_index.txt`, `_concepts.txt` and `_decisions.txt` are dead. Delete them freely.
- The person who runs CI: the step is now `lore adr check`. A wiki repo that copied `examples/wiki-lint.yml` has a job that fails.
- Other repos: nobody outside this repo notices.
